# UX audit — 2026-08-31 (before the reconstruction)

Method: drove the real dev app in a browser as a first-time user (cold start → zoom out →
zoom in → search → select basin → camera preview → dismissal attempts → weather-legend
click → time scrub → motion toggle → phone viewport). Every finding below was observed on
screen, not inferred from code. Screenshots in the session record.

## Findings, in journey order

**F1 — Loading veil has no percentage.** Stage text only ("COMPOSING TERRAIN") with an
indeterminate shimmer. The wait is unquantified; the owner asked for a real progress bar.

**F2 — The reveal exposes a blurry world.** The veil lifted onto low-LOD imagery (soft
blobs for mountains); detail then arrived visibly tile-by-tile. `tilesPending == 0` fires
on a complete-but-coarse pass (and the 18 s timeout also fires on cold caches), so
SCENE_VISUAL_READY as implemented does not mean "coherent opening frame."

**F3 — Oblique default camera.** The opening view is pitched like Google Earth flying, not
a top-down instrument. Terrain/basin relationships read worse tilted.

**F4 — The whole planet is reachable.** Two scroll gestures put the user in a starfield
looking at Central America; Cascadia's domain is a speck. Continental patchwork LODs and
imagery seams dominate. Nothing about the product needs any of this.

**F5 — Basin labels follow you to space.** "CEDAR–LAKE WASHINGTON BASIN" renders over the
open Pacific at planetary range — labels have no maximum-range cull and only 2 of 6 basins
survive collision at orbital anyway.

**F6 — Top bar is developer chrome.** "ORBITAL idle" (band + flight-state), "motion
system→full" (accessibility plumbing as a primary control), an unexplained amber health
dot. None of these are product concepts.

**F7 — Search results expose internal IDs.** Subtitles are `basin:skagit` and
`fp:nwps:MVEW1 · basin:skagit`.

**F8 — The basin panel appears mid-flight**, fully populated, while the camera is still
over Central America. Data before context; the arrival moment has no choreography.

**F9 — The basin panel is a jargon wall.** In one card: eyebrow `BASIN · basin:skagit`;
"regulation: regulated upper · valid … UTC (now)"; "LOW SUSCEPTIBILITY 5 pct" with
✷EXPERIMENTAL as the loudest element; "LEVEL 1,170 cfs 0.29× seasonal p95 reference"
△DERIVED; "CHANGE ↔ steady · 0.98× / 24 h" △DERIVED; "FORCING LOW 11.6 mm / 72 h"
✷EXPERIMENTAL; "HAZARD · NONE official · 72 h" ◆OFFICIAL FORECAST. Five truth-class
badges and six terms of art before any human sentence.

**F10 — Camera preview card is not glass.** Flat dark rectangle, sharp corners, no blur —
visibly a different material from every panel. Its metadata line says "tier A · why"
(internal tiering) in ~9 px text.

**F11 — Click-away does not dismiss anything.** Clicking open water leaves the camera card
open. The provenance popover also survives outside clicks. Escape closes the popover but
NOT the camera card. Every surface has its own (wrong) dismissal rules.

**F12 — Clicking the MODELED legend chip dumps raw contract onto the screen.** A
provenance popover opens DOWNWARD from the bottom-anchored legend, pushing the entire app
up (document scrolls; chrome half-vanishes) and filling half the viewport with:
"product:snodas-swe-daily … (method:field-raster-window@1.0.0) — STUB FIXTURE", "TRUTH B ·
authoritative model", "RAW ARTIFACT —". The user thought they were toggling/inspecting a
weather layer. There is no viewport-aware positioning, no plain-language summary, no
layer on/off control anywhere.

**F13 — Seven timestamps at once while scrubbing.** AS-OF banner (top), AS-OF chip
(bottom-left), timeline readout (UTC + local), window start, window end, RAIN date, SNOW
date. Three of them are the same instant. The AS-OF banner also overlaps the basin panel.

**F14 — Phone layout is z-order chaos.** The AS-OF banner covers the wrapped top-strip
chips; the camera card hangs half off-screen (anchored to a world point, never clamped);
legend chips z-fight between the card and the sheet; the disclaimer floats over the
timeline capsule.

**F15 — Imagery seams persist at basin scale** (bright rectangular NAIP-campaign steps,
soft-vs-sharp LOD borders during every arrival). Separately catalogued in
BasemapProvider.ts as source-data characteristics vs loading behavior — but the USER
cannot tell the difference, and both read as "tiles."

**F16 — Rivers are constant cyan centerlines.** They say "a river exists"; nothing about
state. Physical geography and hydrologic state are one undifferentiated stroke (widths do
band-step after the previous pass, but there is no water register vs state register).

**F17 — Motion toggle did not visibly reshuffle labels in this test** (owner reports it
does under some conditions). Needs a code-level explanation: the flight-end camera differs
between full/reduced (animated settle vs cut), which lands collision at different
projected positions — plausible mechanism for "names changed when I changed motion."

## Systemic diagnoses (what the findings share)

- **No dismissal primitive** — every floating surface hand-rolls lifecycle (F11, F12).
- **No surface primitive** — glass is a per-selector style, so new surfaces (camera card)
  miss it (F10); z-order and clamping are per-component accidents (F13, F14).
- **Truth classes are rendered as headline typography** instead of quiet source
  affordances (F9, F12, F7).
- **Readiness ≠ visual coherence** (F1, F2) and **camera freedom ≠ product scope** (F3,
  F4, F5).
- **Time is displayed wherever a component happens to live** rather than owned by one
  temporal surface (F13).
