Cost classes used below (relative to the current frame; there is no measured budget for
these yet — PERFORMANCE.md §2 targets apply):

- **A** — a uniform/flag; no extra pass. Free.
- **B** — extra work inside the globe shader, or one extra full-screen pass. Cheap at nadir.
- **C** — multiple full-screen passes, depth-dependent, or a resolution multiplier. Scales with pixels.
- **D** — continuous per-frame animation that defeats `requestRenderMode`. Battery-hostile by construction.

Verdicts: **ADOPT** (do it), **TUNE** (already on; decide the value deliberately),
**DEFER** (real, fits, not now), **REJECT** (does not fit a pure-nadir analytical map or
violates the truth doctrine).

## 8. "Flat map" feel at nadir — SceneMode / projection / orthographic

### The geometry, with numbers (fov 60° on the wide axis, WGS84 mean radius 6,371 km)

| Camera height | Ground half-width in frame (flat-plane `h·tan 30°` / along the sphere) | Surface tilt at frame edge (curvature) | Perspective footprint growth at edge, flat-plane term | Extra from curvature, orthographic camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,250 km (ceiling) | 722 km / 749 km | 6.7° | ×1.33 (= 1/cos²30°) | ×1.007 |
| 1,150 km (home) | 664 km / 687 km | 6.2° | ×1.33 | ×1.006 |
| 450 km (basin) | 260 km / 263 km | 2.4° | ×1.33 | ×1.001 |
| 90 km (river) | 52 km / 52 km | 0.5° | ×1.33 | ×1.000 |

(Verification 2026-09-01 recomputed the table: tilt γ from `sin(γ+30°) = (R+h)/R · sin 30°`, along-sphere half-width `R·γ`, ortho extra `1/cos γ`. The original "ground half-width" column was the flat-plane figure only; the along-sphere value is added. All other numbers reproduce.)

Reading: **the "curvature artifact" at max-out is overwhelmingly perspective, not curvature.**
A 60° perspective frustum makes ground pixels at the frame edge ~33 % larger than at the
centre *even over a flat plane*; the sphere adds another ~7 % at the ceiling and nothing at
river band. The second artifact is the **keystone of the domain rectangle**: seen from 3D
nadir, the HARD_DOMAIN's north edge is cos(51.5°)/cos(44°) = **0.865× the length of its
south edge**, and its meridian edges converge by 11.5°·sin(47.75°) ≈ **8.5°** — the
"square-edged vignette" is rendered as a trapezoid with curved top and bottom. Neither a
Cesium knob nor the vignette PNG can straighten that in 3D; only the projection can.

## 9. Not publicly possible / not applicable in 1.144 (verified absent — do not chase)

1. **Per-tile imagery fade** — see prior doc §1c (still true; nothing in the render lens changes it).
2. **A custom shader on the globe other than `Globe.material`.** `CustomShader` is a `Model`/`Cesium3DTileset` feature; the globe's only hook is the material fabric (`APPLY_MATERIAL`) [src:Shaders/GlobeFS.js:143-146,420-430].
3. **Per-layer post-processing.** `PostProcessStage.selected` filters by *picked primitives* (models/3D Tiles); imagery layers are not selectable. A stage always grades the whole frame.
4. **Water without a water mask.** `showWaterEffect` is gated on `terrainProvider.hasWaterMask` [src:GlobeSurfaceTileProvider.js:2389]; the live pyramid has none.
5. **Shadows beyond 5 km without re-tuning the cascade** — `maximumDistance` default 5,000 m [src:Scene/ShadowMap.js:137]; raising it degrades resolution across all cascades.
6. **A built-in spline camera "player"** — splines are evaluators only.
7. **Fog/atmosphere with a stable look across bands** — both are functions of camera distance/height by design [src:Scene/Fog.js:136-150]; a constant grade must come from the layer uniforms or a custom stage.
8. **Rendering at physical pixels without the tile-load cost** — SSE is measured in drawing-buffer pixels, so DPR-true rendering requests more/finer tiles (INFERENCE; measure with `tilesPending`).

## 10. Shortlist — the 6 highest-impact, lowest-risk moves

Ordered by (visible gain ÷ risk). Each is ≤ 10 lines in `SceneController` and reversible.

1. **Turn the two hidden tints off and make the world's colour a design decision.**
   `globe.showGroundAtmosphere = false`; `scene.fog.enabled = false` (or keep culling with
   `fog.visualDensityScalar = 0`). Today state/basin/river bands are washed by distance fog
   and scattering that the grade in `BasemapProvider.ts` was tuned *around* without knowing.
   Cost A. Risk: the grade may now look slightly stronger — re-tune `IMAGERY_GRADE` once,
   against the same screenshots. Hygiene in the same commit: `sun.show = moon.show = false`,
   `globe.showWaterEffect = false`, and an explicit `msaaSamples`.

2. **`useBrowserRecommendedResolution = false` on `high`/`ultra`.** The satellite map is
   drawn at 1× on 2× displays right now; this is the largest sharpness gain available and
   needs no design work. Cost C (fill rate + finer tiles) — gate by quality tier, drop MSAA
   to 1 when DPR ≥ 2, and measure `flight_frame_time` and `tilesPending` before/after.

3. **Orthographic frustum in 3D** (`camera.switchToOrthographicFrustum()` after the initial
   view). Removes the ×1.33 edge-footprint gradient and the edge parallax that make max-out
   read as a globe close-up; leaves every controller intact. Cost A. Risk: patch
   `narrowestHalfAngle()`/`framingRange` for the ortho case before shipping, and re-check
   band thresholds (heights are unchanged, but the *apparent* zoom at a given height is
   wider under ortho, so the band ↔ scale table may want a retune).

4. **Explicit rendering** (`requestRenderMode = true`). A still nadir map should cost
   nothing at rest; the repo already calls `requestRender()` at its mutation points. Cost A
   (negative). Risk: any missed mutation shows as a "stuck" frame — the e2e harness's
   screenshot diffs and the `tilesPending` attribute catch it; ship behind an env flag.

5. **Cap flight apex** with `maximumHeight` (≤ `ZOOM_CEILING_M`) on `flyToBoundingSphere`.
   A basin-to-basin flight must never rise above the composed home framing mid-arc, where
   the discard boundary and vignette edge would enter the frame. Cost A. Risk: nil; the
   flight path simply flattens.

6. **Fixed-sun hillshade at basin/river/local** — `globe.enableLighting = true` with
   `scene.light = new DirectionalLight({ direction: <NW, ~45° elevation>, intensity ~1 })`,
   toggled in `applyBand`, optionally `verticalExaggeration ≈ 1.3`. Relief is the one
   *true* thing a nadir map cannot otherwise show, and the pyramid already carries the
   normals it needs — **but the client must first request them**:
   `CesiumTerrainProvider.fromUrl(root, { requestVertexNormals: true })` in
   `upgradeTerrain()` (verification 2026-09-01; without it `hasVertexNormals` is false and
   lighting silently takes the day/night-terminator path). Cost B. Risk: it darkens the already sun-lit NAIP tone and stacks with
   the imagery's own shadows — must be A/B'd on Skagit and Puyallup screenshots, and it is
   incompatible with the SCENE2D end state (§8), so decide §8 first.

Explicitly **not** on the list: HDR/tonemapping, bloom, AO, DOF, clouds, particles, water,
shadows, clock-driven sun, camera tours — each fails either "nothing breaks the illusion"
or the visual-truth doctrine, or does nothing at nadir.
